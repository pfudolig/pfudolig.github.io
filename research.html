VaDAR

Varstrometry for Dual AGN using Radio Interferometry is a project that leverages the exquisite positional accuracy of the Gaia space telescope with the high spatial resolution of the Very Large Array and the Very Long Baseline Array radio interferometers to detect astrometrically variable quasars as a method of searching for dual AGN candidates.

Publications

Schwartzman, E., Fudolig, P., et al. Varstrometry for Dual AGN using Radio Interferometry: VaDAR with the VLBA, 2025, ApJ, 987, 200

RQQs

Radio-quiet quasars make up approximately 90% of the quasar population, yet require much higher sensitivity and spatial resolution to be detected compared to their brighter radio-loud counterparts. As a result, characterizations of the radio-quiet quasar class are ongoing. With this project, we study how different physical mechanisms and photometric variabilities manifest in quasars of varying radio loudness, using radio data from the Very Large Array and Very Long Baseline Array.
